OVERALL EVIDENCE HIERARCHY
======================================================================

ARG1-puerarin:
Highest computational support among the three pairs.
Alternative ARG1 co-crystal validation using 3KV2/NNH passed in three seeds,
and puerarin top poses were highly reproducible.
Still not experimental binding evidence.

LCN2-linoleate:
Exploratory only.
The co-crystal DKA global redocking protocol failed despite a stable
local crystal-pose optimization.
Candidate pose reproducibility does not rescue the failed validation.

LTF-linoleate:
Exploratory only.
No fatty-acid co-crystal validation was available.
Controls showed overlapping scores and incomplete pose convergence.
No linoleate selectivity claim is supported.
