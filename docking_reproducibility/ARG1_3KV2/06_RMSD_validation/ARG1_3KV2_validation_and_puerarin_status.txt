ARG1 3KV2 / NNH redocking validation and puerarin docking status

Original planned structure:
PDB 4HWW, human ARG1 with co-crystal ligand X7A and two catalytic Mn2+ ions.

4HWW/X7A limitation:
X7A contains boron.
Meeko generated ligand atom type B, but stock AutoDock Vina 1.2.7 rejected
atom type B as an invalid AutoDock atom type.
AutoGrid4 was not available for a custom AutoDock4 boron parameter workflow.
Therefore, 4HWW/X7A redocking was not forced through an unsupported atom-type
substitution and was not used as the formal validation system.

Formal ARG1 validation structure:
PDB 3KV2, human arginase-1 complexed with
N(omega)-hydroxy-nor-L-arginine (NNH; nor-NOHA).
X-ray resolution = 1.55 Angstrom.
Protein chain = A.
Catalytic metals retained:
MN A:514 and MN A:515.
Waters, chain B, the co-crystal ligand, and other heteroatoms were removed
from the docking receptor.

Reference ligand:
NNH A:901.
The crystallographic ligand coordinates were preserved.
Explicit hydrogens were added without moving heavy atoms.
Ligand formal charge = 0.

Validation decision:
PASS.
The best-ranked pose reproduced the crystallographic NNH pose below the
predefined 2.0 Angstrom threshold in all three independent runs.

Scope of validation:
This validates the specified 3KV2/NNH ARG1 docking protocol.
It does not constitute a successful 4HWW/X7A redocking validation.
The substitution of 3KV2/NNH for 4HWW/X7A must be disclosed.

Permitted reporting:
The 3KV2/NNH protocol passed three-seed co-crystal redocking validation.
Puerarin produced a highly reproducible candidate pose within this protocol.
The pose may be used for interaction-hypothesis generation.

Prohibited reporting:
Do not state that 4HWW/X7A redocking passed.
Do not describe the docking score as measured binding affinity.
Do not claim experimentally confirmed ARG1-puerarin binding.
Do not claim direct target engagement or causal therapeutic mechanism.
Do not base the conclusion on docking score alone.
