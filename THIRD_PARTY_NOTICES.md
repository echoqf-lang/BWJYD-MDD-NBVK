## STRING-derived network files

Files under `data/network_inputs/` contain processed information derived from STRING. Users should cite STRING and comply with the current STRING terms for reuse and redistribution. STRING functional associations do not necessarily represent direct physical protein-protein binding.

## Protein Data Bank structures

Raw or processed structural files derived from PDB entries 3KV2, 1QQS, and 1LFG are retained under `docking_reproducibility/` for reproducibility. Users should cite the corresponding structure records and the RCSB Protein Data Bank and follow the applicable PDB data-use policies.

## PubChem compound records

Compound structures derived from PubChem records, including puerarin (CID 5281807), linoleic acid/linoleate (CID 5280450), palmitic acid/palmitate (CID 985), and stearic acid/stearate (CID 5281), remain attributable to PubChem and their original depositors. Users should retain the compound identifiers and cite PubChem when reusing these records.

## GEO transcriptomic datasets

Raw GEO expression files are not redistributed in this repository. Users should retrieve them from the original GEO accessions and comply with the corresponding study and repository terms.
